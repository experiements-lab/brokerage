Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Public institutions like SEFA support micro-loans from R50k up to R5 million. The National Empowerment Fund (NEF) and IDC start at R1 million up to R50 million+. Major commercial banks offer business loans from R500k upwards based on trading history and security. Funding limits depend directly on operational cash flow viability, trading history, and credit profiles.

Lenders evaluate the '5 Cs of Credit': Cash Flow viability (ability to service debt), Collateral/Security, Capability (team experience), Compliance (tax clearance, registry), and Commitment (own contribution/equity).

While no advisory desk can promise external approval, our structured compliance check and document packaging produce a 94% approval rate by ensuring credit committees receive all required information in the format they demand.

Usually, we require CIPC company registration forms, 6-12 months bank statements, director IDs, tax clearance status pin, and any current financial statements or sales contracts. We audit these during our intake consultation.
