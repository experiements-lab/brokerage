Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Funding ranges vary depending on the type of capital and your business profile. Smaller facilities can start from as little as R50,000, while larger growth or development funding can exceed R50 million. During your consultation, our advisors will assess your business and match you to the most suitable funding range within our network.

Lenders evaluate the '5 Cs of Credit': Cash Flow viability (ability to service debt), Collateral/Security, Capability (team experience), Compliance (tax clearance, registry), and Commitment (own contribution/equity).

While no advisory desk can promise external approval, our structured compliance check and document packaging produce an 84% approval rate by ensuring capital providers receive all required information in the format they demand.

Usually, we require CIPC company registration forms, 6-12 months bank statements, director IDs, tax clearance status pin, and any current financial statements or sales contracts. We audit these during our intake consultation.
